\section{Comparison of REMIND 12-region and country-level resolution}

The MFA can be computed either on the REMIND 12-region aggregation (h12) or on country level (iso249).
The following figures compare both resolutions for the SSP2 scenario.
Results computed on country level are aggregated to the REMIND 12 regions after the computation
(iso249 $\rightarrow$ h12).
The dashed vertical line marks the last historical year.
