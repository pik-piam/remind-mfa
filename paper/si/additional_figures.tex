
\section{Additional Figures} \label{sec:add_figures}

This section shows the results figures of the main paper (Section~\ref{MS-sec:results}) for all materials, and additionally in the REMIND 12-region aggregation it was computed with: the regional comparison (Figure~\ref{MS-fig:demand}, Subsection \ref{sec:add_fig_5}), the Sankey diagrams (Figure~\ref{MS-fig:sankey_carbon}, Subsection \ref{sec:add_fig_6}), trade (Figure~\ref{MS-fig:trade_steel}, Subsection \ref{sec:add_fig_7}), and the scenario comparison (Figure~\ref{MS-fig:scenarios_cement}, Subsection \ref{sec:add_fig_8}). The figures from the main paper are repeated here for completeness and side-by-side comparison.
The exogenous model inputs GDP per capita and population are shown in Subsections \ref{sec:add_gdppc} and \ref{sec:add_pop}.
Unless stated otherwise, all results refer to the SSP2 scenario, computed on REMIND 12-region resolution.

The MFA can be computed with arbitrary regional aggregation, and demand trajectories are provided with the REMIND 12-region aggregation (h12) and with country level (iso249) resolution.
The resolution of the MFA computation can have an effect on the results, even if the results are aggregated to the same regional aggregation after the computation. For details on these effects, see the README in the data repository.
Two subsections illustrate the effect of different regional aggregations on the results: Subsection \ref{sec:add_fig_5_countries} shows the results of Figure~\ref{MS-fig:demand} for a model run with the MFA computed on country level, and aggregated to the respective regions after the computation. Subsection \ref{sec:comparison_country_level} directly compares production and per-capita in-use stocks for both resolutions, with one panel per region.

In all time series plots, the dashed vertical line marks the last historical year of the respective material.
